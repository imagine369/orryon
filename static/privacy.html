Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

We built Orryon with a strong focus on privacy and user control. Your data belongs to you.

Orryon is designed local-first. When self-hosted or used locally, your data never leaves your device. In hosted mode, we only collect what is necessary and do not sell or monetize your personal data.

Self-Hosted vs Hosted

Self-hosted/Local: All data stays in finance.db on your device. We have no access.

Hosted SaaS: We store your data securely with optional encryption and provide full export and deletion tools.

AI and Wellness Features

When using the AI, limited context is sent to xAI's Grok. The Breathe and Meditation features are for general relaxation and wellness only. Please see our Terms of Service for important disclaimers about these features.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not use tracking analytics or advertising networks
  • We do not share your data for marketing purposes

Third-Party Services

  • xAI Grok: Used for AI capabilities
  • Stripe: Secure payment processing
  • SMTP (optional): Transactional emails through your own provider

Your Rights

  • Full data export at any time
  • Permanent account and data deletion
  • Complete control when self-hosting

Security

We use secure authentication, optional encryption, and HTTPS for hosted instances. Self-hosted users are responsible for their own security.

We genuinely want Orryon to be a helpful, privacy-first tool that puts you in control. Your trust matters to us.
