Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

orryon is a fully local, privacy-first personal finance and productivity tool. This page explains exactly what we do — and don't — do with your data.

Your data stays on your device

Everything you add to orryon — transactions, budgets, goals, calendar events, grocery lists, notes, and chat history — is stored only in a local SQLite file called finance.db on your own device. We do not run a cloud server that collects or stores your personal data.

What we store locally

  • Email address: If you sign in with OTP, your email is saved in the local finance.db to identify your account across sessions. It never leaves your device.
  • Financial & personal data: All transactions, budgets, goals, events, notes, and chat history are stored only in finance.db on your device.

We do not transmit, collect, or store any of this data on any server.

What we do not do

  • We do not track you across websites or devices.
  • We do not use advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any user data — ever.
  • We do not have access to your data.

The one thing that leaves your device

When you chat with orryon, your message is sent to the xAI Grok API so the AI can understand and respond. That's it. Please review xAI's Privacy Policy for how they handle those messages.

Email notifications (optional)

If you choose to configure SMTP reminders, emails are sent directly from your own email account credentials. We never see the content of those emails.

Shared dashboard links

You can generate a read-only share link to your Dashboard. Anyone with that link can view your financial overview. The data still comes from your local database — we do not host or store a copy of it.

Your responsibilities

  • Back up your finance.db file regularly.
  • Keep your device and its contents secure.
  • Protect your xAI API key.
  • Understand that shared links expose your data to whoever has the link.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated date at the top of this page.

orryon was built with one core principle:
Your data belongs to you, not to us. No cloud servers. No tracking. Your email stays local.
