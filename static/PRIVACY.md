# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: April 11, 2026**

Orryon ("we", "us", or "our") is a fully local, privacy-first personal finance and productivity tool.

### What This Means

- **All your data stays on your device.** Your transactions, goals, schedule, notes, and chat history are stored locally in a SQLite database file (`finance.db`).
- We **do not** run a server that collects or stores your personal data.
- The only information that ever leaves your computer is the text you send to the Grok API (provided by xAI) when you chat with Orryon.

### Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect your name, email, financial data, or usage analytics.
- We do not track you across websites or devices.
- We do not sell or share any user data.

### Third-Party Services

- **xAI Grok API**: When you use the AI chat, your messages are sent to xAI. Please review xAI's [privacy policy](https://x.ai/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Email Notifications** (optional): If you configure SMTP for reminders, emails are sent directly from your own email account.

### Your Responsibilities

- You are responsible for backing up your `finance.db` file.
- You are responsible for keeping your xAI API key secure.
- Shared dashboard links (read-only) expose your financial overview to anyone with the link.

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

---

**Orryon is built for privacy. Your data belongs to you.**
